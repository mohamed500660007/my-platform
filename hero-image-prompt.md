# برومبت صورة الـ Hero Section — منصة «بــن نــاجــح»

> **طريقة الاستخدام:** افتح ChatGPT، ارفع صورتك الشخصية بأعلى دقة عندك، والصق البرومبت اللي تحت كما هو.
> استخدم موديل توليد/تعديل الصور (image input + generation).

---

## ١ · البرومبت الأساسي — انسخه كما هو

```text
ROLE
You are a senior Adobe Photoshop art director and retoucher building a hero
image for a premium Arabic EdTech landing page. Work like a real photo
compositor: cut-outs, layer masks, printed-paper textures, hard offset
shadows — not like an AI illustration generator.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
⛔ RULE #0 — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE. READ IT 3 TIMES.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
The attached photo is a REAL PERSON. His face is NOT to be regenerated.

• DO NOT change the facial features. DO NOT change the facial features.
  DO NOT change the facial features.
• DO NOT beautify, smooth, slim, reshape, or "improve" anything.
• DO NOT alter: eye shape, eye color, eyebrows, nose, mouth, lips, jawline,
  cheekbones, chin, forehead, ears, skin tone, skin texture, moles, freckles,
  facial hair / beard / moustache shape and density, hairline, hair style,
  glasses (keep the exact same frames), age, weight, or expression.
• Treat the head and face as a LOCKED, UNTOUCHABLE pixel region. Copy it from
  the source photo 1:1. You may ONLY:
     – cut it out from its background (clean edge mask, keep hair detail),
     – scale/position it,
     – add a very subtle global color-grade so it sits in the scene
       (max ±5% warmth/exposure — nothing that shifts identity).
• If you cannot preserve the face exactly, STOP and return the composition
  with the original face pasted unmodified rather than inventing a new one.
⚠️ لا تغيّر ملامح الوجه إطلاقًا. الوجه يُنسخ كما هو من الصورة المرفقة بدون
   أي تعديل أو تجميل أو إعادة رسم. ده أهم شرط في الطلب كله.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
THE BRAND — "بــن نــاجــح" (Ben Nagah)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
An Arabic (RTL) educational platform for Egyptian 2nd-year Baccalaureate
students, subject: Programming & Artificial Intelligence. Its whole visual
identity is "a real handwritten study notebook, photographed from above" —
warm ivory paper, navy ink, five highlighter pens, washi tape, torn edges.

EXACT COLOR PALETTE — use these hex values only, nothing else:
  Paper / background ...... #FFFCF2  →  #FBF5E4 (soft vertical gradient)
  Ink (all dark lines/text) #1E2A46
  Soft ink (secondary) .... #5C6683
  Highlighter · Sun ....... #FFC93C   (dark ink on it: #6B4B00)
  Highlighter · Berry ..... #FF4D6D   (#7A0C24)
  Highlighter · Mint ...... #00C2A8   (#005247)
  Highlighter · Violet .... #7A5CFF   (#2E1C7A)
  Highlighter · Sky ....... #2FA8FF   (#0A4C7D)
NO other colors. No teal-orange grade, no neon, no purple-blue AI gradient
mush, no glow, no lens flare, no bokeh particles.

SIGNATURE STYLE RULES (these are what make it look like the brand):
1. HARD SHADOWS ONLY. Every floating element casts a solid offset shadow
   with ZERO blur — e.g. 8px right / 10px down of rgba(30,42,70,0.14).
   This is the print/sticker look. Soft blurry drop shadows are forbidden.
2. Every object is rotated slightly, between −1.5° and +3°. Nothing is
   perfectly straight. It must feel placed by hand, not snapped to a grid.
3. Organic corner radii — corners are uneven (e.g. 22px / 7px / 22px / 7px),
   like they were drawn, never uniform rounded rectangles.
4. Paper everywhere: visible fiber grain, a faint 5mm dot-grid, one thin red
   margin rule, and real torn/ripped paper edges with white fibrous fray.
5. Washi tape strips holding things down — diagonal candy stripes in Sun
   (#FFC93C) and Mint (#00C2A8), and a Berry (#FF4D6D) strip with tiny white
   polka dots. Tape is slightly translucent with a soft sheen at its top edge.
6. Highlighter marks are imperfect: they overshoot the word, the stroke
   starts and ends translucent, like a real marker pressed on paper.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
THE COMPOSITION — "The Exploded Study Desk"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
A top-down-ish, 3/4 "flat-lay meets floating collage" scene. Think a study
desk that has been gently exploded upward into layered floating planes,
photographed with a real camera on a tripod.

FOREGROUND — THE SUBJECT:
The man from the attached photo, cut out cleanly as a paper sticker: a 4px
crisp white paper border traces his silhouette (classic Photoshop
sticker/cutout treatment), with a hard navy offset shadow beneath it, tilted
about −2°. He is positioned on the LEFT THIRD of the frame, from mid-chest
up, arms relaxed, calm confident expression — EXACTLY the expression and face
from the source photo, unchanged. He wears a simple clean shirt in a neutral
tone. A short strip of Mint washi tape sticks the top corner of his cutout to
the scene, as if he were pinned onto the page.

BEHIND HIM — THE ANCHOR SHAPE:
A large hand-drawn imperfect circle (a "burst") filled with a Sun gradient
(#FFE9A6 → #FFC93C → #FFAA10), outlined with a 3px navy ink stroke, offset by
a solid Berry (#FF4D6D) duplicate shadow 10px right / 11px down — like a
misprinted risograph. Its edge carries a subtle halftone dot texture. It is
rotated −5° and sits directly behind his head and shoulders, haloing him.

MID LAYER — FLOATING NOTEBOOK ARTIFACTS (each a separate plane, each with a
hard shadow, each rotated a few degrees, arranged in a loose diagonal rhythm
that opens toward the right):
  • Two or three ivory A4 notebook sheets seen at a slight angle, with a red
    margin line, punched binder holes, dot-grid, and handwritten-looking
    Arabic-shaped ink marks (abstract, illegible, calligraphic rhythm only —
    do NOT attempt real readable Arabic words).
  • A small ivory sticky note in Mint tint with a torn bottom edge.
  • A sticky note in Sky tint with a hand-drawn checkbox and a navy tick.
  • A Berry-tinted note with a small hand-drawn exclamation badge.
  • A modern smartphone, screen facing us, tilted −3°, its screen showing a
    clean ivory app UI with a navy top bar and four pastel lesson rows and a
    tiny rainbow progress bar — small, out of focus enough to read as texture
    but clearly a phone.
  • Hand-drawn ink doodles floating between the planes, drawn like a student's
    ballpoint pen: a small neural-network node diagram, a binary "1010"
    fragment, a lightbulb, a circuit trace, a curved arrow, a tiny cloud icon,
    a spiral. Thin, wobbly, human line quality — NOT vector-perfect icons.
  • Real highlighter swipes in Sun and Mint laid across two of the sheets.
  • Two or three washi tape strips holding sheets to the paper backdrop.

BACKGROUND:
Warm ivory paper (#FFFCF2 → #FBF5E4), with a faint dot grid and gentle paper
grain. Soft, broad, natural window light coming from the upper right, giving
everything an honest, slightly warm cast. Keep the RIGHT 40% of the frame
calm and mostly empty — clean paper with only one or two small floating
elements — because Arabic headline text will be placed there. Do NOT put any
text, letters, numbers or logos in the image.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PHOTOGRAPHY / REALISM DIRECTION (anti-AI look)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
• Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, f/5.6 — deep, honest focus.
  Everything readable, no dreamy blur, no cinematic vignette.
• Real materials: you can see paper fiber, the slight cockle of a sheet, the
  matte micro-texture of tape, the tiny fuzz on a torn edge, the faint
  impression of pen pressure.
• Natural skin: keep visible pores, real texture, tiny imperfections. Zero
  skin smoothing. Zero airbrushing. Zero "AI plastic sheen."
• Light behaves consistently — one light source, one shadow direction for the
  real 3D objects, while the flat graphic elements keep their stylized hard
  offset shadows. That contrast is intentional and is the whole art direction.
• Subtle analog grain over the entire image. No HDR crunch, no over-sharpening
  halos, no oversaturation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
OUTPUT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
• Aspect ratio 4:5 (portrait) — or 1:1 if 4:5 is unavailable.
• Highest available resolution, maximum detail, crisp edges.
• No text, no watermark, no signature, no UI chrome, no borders.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
FINAL SELF-CHECK BEFORE YOU RENDER — answer all as YES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Is the face pixel-identical in features to the attached photo? (MUST be YES)
2. Did I avoid smoothing, slimming, or re-drawing any part of his face? (YES)
3. Are all shadows on the graphic elements ZERO-blur hard offsets? (YES)
4. Are all colors strictly from the listed hex palette? (YES)
5. Is the right 40% of the frame left calm for Arabic text? (YES)
6. Is there NO readable text anywhere in the image? (YES)
7. Does it look like a photographed paper collage, not an AI render? (YES)
```

## ٢ · اتجاهات بديلة

بدّل فقرة **THE COMPOSITION** بس بواحدة من دول، وسيب باقي البرومبت زي ما هو.

### البديل ١ — «الطالب جوّه الورقة»

```text
THE COMPOSITION — "Stepping Out of the Page"
The subject stands inside a giant open notebook page that curves up behind him
like a stage backdrop. Its dot-grid and red margin line bend in perspective.
He steps out of the page's torn edge into real space — his left half is flat
printed paper (halftone, ink-limited, slightly desaturated), his right half is
a real photograph with full depth. The transition line is a genuine ripped
paper edge with white fibrous fray. Highlighter strokes and washi tape climb
the curved page around him. Keep the right 40% of the frame calm for text.
```

### البديل ٢ — «خريطة المنهج المعلّقة»

```text
THE COMPOSITION — "The Curriculum Wall"
The subject stands at the right-of-centre, looking left at a hanging wall of
ivory index cards connected by thin navy string — a physical "curriculum map."
Four colour-coded card clusters (Violet, Berry, Sky, Mint) represent the four
units of the term. Two cards are flipped face-up and lit warmly; the rest wait
in cooler shadow. Brass push-pins, washi tape, and hand-drawn ink arrows
connect them. Depth is created by cards hanging at three different distances
from camera. Keep the left 35% of the frame calm for text.
```

### البديل ٣ — «مكتب المذاكرة بالليل»

```text
THE COMPOSITION — "The Late Study Desk"
A warm overhead 3/4 view of a real wooden study desk at night, lit by one desk
lamp from the upper right. On it: the open Ben Nagah notebook (ivory, dot-grid,
red margin, highlighter marks), a phone showing the app, five highlighter pens
scattered, a roll of washi tape, a mug. The subject leans into frame from the
left edge — elbow on the desk, chin resting on his hand, calm and focused,
looking at the notebook. Above the desk, torn paper fragments and hand-drawn
ink doodles float upward out of the page into the dark, each with a hard offset
shadow, as if the ideas are lifting off the paper. Keep the right 40% calm.
```

## ٤ · نصايح وقت الإرسال

1. **ابعت الصورة الأصلية بأعلى دقة عندك.** كل ما الوش يكون واضح وكبير في الفريم، كل ما الحفاظ عليه يبقى أسهل بكتير.
2. **قارن الوش بالأصلي أول ما تيجي النتيجة.** لو اتغيّر أي حاجة، رد عليه بالجملة دي:
   > `The face changed. Redo it and paste the original face from my photo unmodified — cut-out only, no regeneration.`
3. **اطلب نسخة تانية بدون الوش** (نفس الكولاج بس فاضي من الشخص). كده تقدر تركّب صورتك بنفسك في فوتوشوب وتضمن الوش ١٠٠٪.
4. **لو الوش طلع صغير أو مش واضح،** قوله: `Make the subject larger — head and shoulders should occupy at least 35% of the frame height.`
